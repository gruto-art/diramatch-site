Live in Israel today

A working listing network, run mostly by software.

Dira Match started as a simple problem. In Israel, apartments are sold and rented through WhatsApp groups and word of mouth. We built a pipeline that takes a listing from the owner, checks it, and publishes it to our community channels with photos and a contact flow. One human approval step stays in the loop.

  1. OwnerSubmits the property

    A web form with details and up to four photos. No account needed.

  2. SystemQueues it for review

    The listing lands in our operations sheet, marked "pending".

  3. TeamApproves with one click

    A person flips the status. That is the only manual step.

  4. SystemPublishes everywhere

    Google Chat community card with a photo grid, plus WhatsApp distribution, under a listing code.

  5. AIHandles the calls

    Calls to our business line are recorded, transcribed in Hebrew and summarized into the CRM.

The agent layer

Agents that build and run the whole process.

Each agent owns one stage of a property's life, from first contact to signed contract. They share a single property record and a single customer history. A person stays in charge of every irreversible step.

Intake

Live

Collects the listing from the owner, normalizes it, and flags missing or suspicious details before a human sees it.

In: owner form, photos
Out: verified property record

Voice

In build

One business number for the team. Every call is recorded, transcribed in Hebrew, summarized, and filed with the next action.

In: phone calls
Out: transcript, summary, follow-up task

Personalizer

Next

Renders the same listing for each reader: language, emphasis, length and channel. The demo above shows the idea.

In: property record + reader profile
Out: message, page or script

Matcher

Next

Learns what each seeker wants from their messages and calls, then reaches out when the right property appears.

In: conversations, saved searches
Out: ranked matches with reasons

Process

Planned

Schedules viewings, gathers documents, tracks offers, and keeps buyer, seller, lawyer and mortgage advisor on one timeline.

In: deal state
Out: reminders, checklists, status for every party

Builder

Planned

Sets up the whole stack for a new market or partner agency (forms, channels, phone line, CRM) from a short brief.

In: market brief
Out: a running local instance
Built on Claude

A two-person team shipping like a twenty-person one.

Dira Match is built and operated with Claude. Our system was written with Claude Code, and Claude models do the language work in production. That is how a small founding team runs a listing network, a CRM and a phone line at once.

  • EngineeringThe whole platform (forms, publishing, payments, CRM, phone line) is developed and maintained with Claude Code.
  • Call intelligenceClaude Haiku turns Hebrew call transcripts into a summary, the customer's intent, city, budget and the next step.
  • OperationsHealth checks, diagnostics and deploys run through Claude agents with defined playbooks.
  • NextListing personalization and buyer matching, in Hebrew and English first.
